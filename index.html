01

PicoRet: Know, Form, Correct for Ultra-Lightweight Low-Light Image Enhancement

Under review at ICASSP 2027 · First author

An ultra-lightweight low-light enhancement method using analytical illumination and chromaticity priors, compact representations, and image-dependent correction. The model has 817 parameters and 0.085G FLOPs.

02

Improving Generalization in Federated Dynamic Sparse Training via Flatness-Aware Topology Adaptation

Under review at AAAI 2027 · First author

FedFLaST combines nominal and perturbation sensitivity to guide sparse topology adaptation, improving generalization and robustness without additional communication.

03

From AI Scientists to AI Scientific Communities: A Survey of Collective AutoResearch

Accepted to Findings of EMNLP 2026 · Co-first author

A survey of collective automated research, examining independent verification, traceable evidence, and scientific communities built around differentiated roles, shared records, and auditing.
