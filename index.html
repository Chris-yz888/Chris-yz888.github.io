About Me

I am a master's student in Computer Science at City University of Hong Kong. I received my bachelor's degree in Information Management and Information Systems from Beijing Institute of Technology in 2025.

My research interests center on efficient computer vision and computational photography. I am currently exploring 3D and 4D vision and generative models.

Outside of research, I enjoy landscape photography and documenting everyday life. I use photography to capture the places I visit and the small moments around me.

Publications

From AI Scientists to AI Scientific Communities: A Survey of Collective AutoResearch

Accepted to Findings of EMNLP 2026 · Co-first author

A survey of collective automated research, examining independent verification, traceable evidence, and scientific communities built around differentiated roles, shared records, and auditing.

My contribution. Proposed the AI scientific communities concept and contributed to the literature review and manuscript.

Research

PicoRet: Know, Form, Correct for Ultra-Lightweight Low-Light Image Enhancement

Under review at ICASSP 2027 · First author

An ultra-lightweight low-light enhancement method using analytical illumination and chromaticity priors, compact representations, and image-dependent correction. The model has 817 parameters and 0.085G FLOPs.

My contribution. Designed the method, conducted baseline and ablation studies on LOL-v1/v2 and cross-dataset evaluation on MEF, LIME, and DICM, and wrote the manuscript.

Code on GitHub↗

Improving Generalization in Federated Dynamic Sparse Training via Flatness-Aware Topology Adaptation

Under review at AAAI 2027 · First author

FedFLaST combines nominal and perturbation sensitivity to guide sparse topology adaptation, improving generalization and robustness without additional communication.

My contribution. Designed the topology adaptation algorithm and conducted image classification, robustness, and ablation experiments across datasets and sparsity levels.

GitHub↗Code coming soon.

FaultBench-Edu: Benchmarking LLMs for Instructional Fault Tolerance in Educational Dialogues

Under review at AAAI 2027 · Co-first author

A benchmark of whether educational dialogue models can recheck and recover from earlier instructional errors, using fault injection, adversarial student challenges, and evolutionary stress testing.

My contribution. Improved fault generation and recovery evaluation, and conducted experiments covering 13 tutoring fault types and approximately 40,000 LLM calls.

EvoMystery: Survival of the Playable in Evolutionary Murder-Mystery Generation

Working paper · Collaborating author

An evolutionary framework for murder-mystery generation that models case truth, character knowledge, and clues, then uses agent playtesting and failure feedback to revise the information structure.

Projects

01

Cross-View Vehicle Image Translation and 3D Reconstruction

Computer Graphics · Individual course project

Rendered 2,984 paired frontal and oblique vehicle views in Blender. Trained a Pix2Pix U-Net/PatchGAN with VGG16 perceptual loss to generate auxiliary views for reconstruction.

02

Transfer Learning with NAFNet for Astrophotography Denoising

Visual and Image Computing · Individual course project

Built a dataset of 5,200 patches from HST/ESO images with Poisson–Gaussian noise and fine-tuned a SIDD-pretrained NAFNet. On the synthetic test set, PSNR/SSIM improved from 31.08/0.8124 to 32.31/0.8557.
